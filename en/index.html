Approach

Geology first, then hardware

Most costly downhole mistakes start with a mistake in the reservoir model. So we start with data: surveys and geophysics produce the model, the model drives the design, and the design drives drilling and workover. One team is accountable for the whole chain.

01 · Data

Geophysics and field surveys

We study the section and reservoir behaviour: geophysical surveys, pressure and flow-rate measurements, fluid sampling.

02 · Model and design

Design documents and development analysis

We prepare basic and technical design documents and analyse field development.

03 · Wellsite operations

From spud to abandonment

We drill, cement, complete and work over wells, and apply enhanced oil recovery methods.
